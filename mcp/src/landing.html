Every session starts at zero

You paste the diff. You re-explain the project. You summarize what you tried yesterday and why it didn't work. Your assistant is brilliant and has no memory of any of it.

Meanwhile the answer was on your screen the whole time: which files you touched, what you read, where the afternoon actually went. Panopticon captures that and hands it to whichever agent asks.

How it works

Record. The app captures periodic screenshots with ScreenCaptureKit, skipping idle stretches and apps you've blocked. Everything lands in a SQLite database on your Mac.

Distill. An LLM you choose (Gemini, Claude Code, Codex, or a local model) batches the captures into timeline cards: what you did, when, in which category, with which apps. A daily recap is generated each morning.

Serve. Every few hours the app pushes day snapshots to a Postgres you own. Your agents read it back over MCP: list days, read a day's timeline, search it, pull the recap, break down time by category.

What your agents can ask

  • get_timeline everything you did on a given day, card by card
  • search_timeline "when did I last touch the auth middleware?"
  • get_recap the generated standup: highlights, tasks, blockers
  • category_breakdown where the hours actually went
  • list_days what's recorded, and how fresh the sync is

Self-host in three steps

No hosted service, no account. The app runs on your Mac, the server on your Vercel and Neon, and one token you generate guards both.

01Deploy the server. One click, then set two env vars: your Neon connection string and a token you make up.

psql "$DATABASE_URL" -f mcp/sql/schema.sql

02Run the app. Grant Screen Recording, then paste your server URL and token into Settings → Export → Cloud sync.

Download Panopticon

03Point your agents at it. Anything that speaks MCP. Swap in your server URL and token:

Claude Code claude mcp add --transport http panopticon https://<your-server>/mcp --header "Authorization: Bearer <token>"

Questions you're probably having

Does my screen get uploaded somewhere? Capture, analysis and storage all happen on your Mac in a local SQLite database. Screenshots leave only as input to the LLM provider you configured, or never, if you point it at a local model. What syncs to your own Postgres is the resulting text timeline, not the images.

What does it cost? The app and server are free and open source. You pay whatever your LLM provider charges for analysis; Neon and Vercel both have free tiers that comfortably fit one person's timeline.

Is it always recording? It skips idle stretches and any apps you've blocked, and pause lives in the menu bar. Recordings and cards are yours to delete at any time.

Who can read the data? Whoever holds the bearer token you generated. Rotate it, or drop the tables, whenever you like.

Do I need all three of Poke, folk and Hermes? No, those are just examples. Anything that speaks MCP can read the timeline.
